How this fits your HSG application

  • Cultural events: HSG can cover all food costs.
  • Programmatic events: food is covered up to 60% of the event budget.
  • Social events: HSG covers up to $400 for each co-hosting HSO.

Paying vendors

  • For Harris funds, confirm the payment route with Student Engagement & Belonging. Ask the vendor for an itemized written quote and invoice terms before committing.
  • University direct payments by check take about two weeks to process. Confirm this applies to your Harris account.
  • For authorized GEMS purchases, use the designated payer and keep the itemized receipt.
  • Ordering Domino's? Call the store instead of ordering online and say it is a tax-exempt University of Chicago order. Have the Illinois sales tax exemption certificate ready; it is on the HSO Resources Canvas site under Financial Support. Check that the receipt shows $0 tax.
  • One order per shared event. Collect dietary needs before the order cutoff and ask the vendor to label dishes separately.
