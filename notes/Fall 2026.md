## What changed this quarter

Fall 2026 is the first quarter under a new, rules-based framework:

* **One published formula sets every allocation.** A flat share plus a flexible share based on score and eligible request. Any committee adjustment needs a written justification and is marked in the report.
* **Food limits are applied event by event**, with a written explanation for every exclusion.
* **There is no per-club cap.** The old $1,800 limit is gone; large allocations are flagged for committee review instead.
* **Every on-time applicant is scored on the same rubric**, including applications that were initially missed.
* **Earmarked funds are set aside.** Money restricted by a sponsor, donor or grant, committed under a signed contract, or saved for a specific, itemized event later in the academic year doesn't count as rollover.
* **HSOs can respond.** They have 72 hours after receiving their event classifications to contest a classification, correct attendance, confirm co-hosts, update rollover or claim earmarked funds.

## Next steps

* Publish the food and event rules before applications open, so HSOs can budget around them.
* Collect actual attendance and spending after each quarter, so future reports compare plans to outcomes.
